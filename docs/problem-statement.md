# Problem Statement

## Background

MissionGuard AI addresses the Mission Readiness & Predictive Maintenance problem in the Defense & Aerospace domain. Organizations may have equipment health data from sensors and maintenance service records, but this information may not be analyzed together to provide a clear view of asset readiness before a mission window.


## The Problem

Maintenance teams may rely on calendar-based maintenance, which can miss early warning signs in sensor data. This makes it difficult to identify assets that are not ready, detect potential failure risks early, understand the reasons behind those risks, and prioritize maintenance actions.

## Who is Affected

The primary users are maintenance and operations teams responsible for monitoring equipment health and preparing assets for upcoming mission windows. They need a clear and explainable way to identify assets requiring attention and prioritize maintenance activities.

## Why It Matters

Poor visibility into asset health can result in maintenance being performed too late or resources being spent on lower-priority assets. Early identification of abnormal conditions can help maintenance teams focus their attention on assets with higher risk and improve readiness planning.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Traditional calendar-based maintenance schedules maintenance according to fixed intervals rather than continuously evaluating current sensor conditions. When sensor readings and maintenance records are considered separately, it can also be difficult to understand the overall readiness of an asset and prioritize the most important maintenance actions.
